Accessibility

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Our aim

We want everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA and the Israeli Standard IS 5568 at level AA.

What we have done

  • The site works with a keyboard alone, the focused element is always clearly marked, and a “Skip to content” link is the first thing the keyboard reaches.
  • Images that carry meaning have text descriptions; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Links that open in a new tab say so to screen readers.
  • Headings, lists and landmarks are marked up so screen readers can move through the page.
  • Text has strong contrast against the background.
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the moving effects are turned off.
  • The home page has a “Pause motion” button in the bottom corner that stops every moving effect. Nothing on the site changes on a timer.
  • The layout adapts to phones, tablets and computers, and the text can be enlarged in the browser.

Known limits

  • Pictures of the game are concept art; their descriptions are short.
  • The waitlist form and our social pages are run by other companies, and their accessibility depends on them.

Contact

If something on the site is hard to use, or you need information in another way, send us a direct message on our Discord server. We will reply as soon as we can.

The developer checked this site on November 13, 2026: moving through every page with the keyboard alone, text contrast, headings and landmarks, and the layout on phone and computer screens. It has not yet been tested by an outside accessibility expert or with every screen reader.
