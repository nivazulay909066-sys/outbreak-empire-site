In one sentence

Outbreak Empire is a zombie strategy game for Android and iOS with no in-app purchases: build a settlement in a ruined city, hold your walls against the hordes, and fight other players for the map.

Description

No one knows how it started. The government and the army were the first to fall, and the old order fell with them. What is left is a world of groups and settlements fighting to survive, gangs and militias that thrive on the chaos, and hordes of the dead that turn toward every sound.

In Outbreak Empire you lead a small settlement in a ruined city. Take in the survivors who reach your gate and put them to work, raise defenses against the hordes that every noise draws in, and defend against or attack the other players who want what is yours. Join a coalition or found your own, plan your wars together, and fight for the places that decide who runs the city.

Pure play to win

Outbreak Empire is made for players who are done with strategy games where you have to pay hundreds of dollars a month just to keep up. The game has no in-app purchases: no packs, no gems, no VIP levels, no shortcuts. No one can buy power, speed or resources. The strongest player is the best player, not the one who spent the most.

Features

  • Survive: a settlement is its people. Rescue survivors and put the right people in the right jobs.
  • Hold the wall: noise draws the dead, and a growing settlement is never quiet.
  • Defend or attack: other settlements on a live map want what is yours.
  • Unite: join a coalition, answer your allies, and plan your wars together.
  • Same rules for all: nothing in the game is for sale.
