The city was evacuated in a hurry. The army held on for a few more weeks, then pulled out — leaving checkpoints, abandoned bases and gear behind.

What's left are small communities, makeshift trading posts, gangs, militias. And the hordes, drawn to every sound.

Nobody knows what caused it. Nobody is coming.

What you'll do

Survival is the game.
Strategy is how you win it.

01

Survivors, not numbers

Every survivor has a trade. Keep them fed, watered, warm and healthy — or watch morale crack.

When winter comes, wood and fuel are the difference between a settlement and a graveyard.

02

Every crate travels

Trade is physical. Goods cross the map by convoy, and any convoy can be hit.

Guard yours. Take theirs. Prisoners can be ransomed.

03

The horde hears you.

The dead are drawn to the noise your settlement makes. They come on their own, and they change over time. Build quiet, build strong — or be ready.

Questions

What is Outbreak Empire?

A multiplayer survival strategy game for mobile. You build a settlement, manage your people and resources, raise an army, trade, fight, and join a coalition, all in one shared world that never resets.

Which platforms?

Android and iOS.

When can I play?

There's no release date yet. Join the waitlist or the Discord to hear first when closed test signups open.

Can I lose everything?

No. Losses hurt, and an army can be wiped out, but your headquarters and the core of your settlement always survive, and there is always a way back.

Who's making it?

A solo indie developer, building it in the open. Follow along on Discord and X.
