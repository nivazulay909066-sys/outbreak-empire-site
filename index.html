The world

The city fell. Nobody came. Take it back.

The evacuation took days. The army held on for a few weeks, then pulled out and left its bases, its checkpoints and its gear behind. What remains is a city of small camps, makeshift markets, gangs and militias, and hordes that turn toward every sound.

You reach the edge of it with a handful of people and nothing to your name. Pick your ground and give it a name. Everything that happens next is your call.

A refuge. A trading power. An empire.

Your survivors are people, not numbers

Each one has a trade, and each one needs clean water, food, heat and a reason to keep going. Put the right people in the right jobs, make the hard calls a leader has to make, and be ready when winter comes.

The dead hear everything

Noise draws them, and a growing settlement is never quiet. Hordes come at your walls on their own, and they change as time passes. Raise defenses, train fighters, and send hunting parties out to bring back what the dead were carrying.

What sets it apart

One world. No resets.

  • A world that stays

    The world never resets. What you build today is still standing next month, and next year.

  • Everything is on the map

    Armies, convoys and hordes all move in real time, in plain sight. Scout them, intercept them, or lay the ambush.

  • Weather that matters

    Rain, storms and snow change your plans. When winter hits, heat becomes one more thing your people can run out of.

  • Losses that sting

    Lose a fight and you will feel it. But no settlement is ever wiped off the map, so there is always a way back.

  • Prisoners and ransom

    Fighters you capture are worth something to the people who lost them. Name your price for sending them home.

  • Minutes or hours

    Drop in for a few minutes to set things moving, or settle in for a long session when a war is on.

FAQ

Questions

What kind of game is Outbreak Empire?

A zombie strategy game for Android and iOS, set in a city that fell to an outbreak. At heart it is a survival city builder: you build a settlement, look after the people in it, raise an army and hunt the dead. It is also a multiplayer strategy game, because you trade and fight for a live map you share with other players.

When does Outbreak Empire come out?

There is no launch date yet. A closed test comes first. Join the waitlist and we will email you as soon as there is news.

Which devices will it run on?

Outbreak Empire is a post-apocalyptic mobile game, being made for Android and iOS.

Will it be free?

Yes. Outbreak Empire will be free.

What is the closed test?

An early version of the game, played by a limited group before launch so we can find problems and tune the balance. We will invite players from the waitlist. Joining the waitlist does not guarantee a place.

Do I play alone?

No. Every settlement shares the world with other players. Trade with them, fight them, or join a coalition and take on what no single settlement could.

Does the world reset?

No. There is one lasting world, and what you build in it stays built.

Can I lose my settlement?

You can lose fights, supplies and ground, and it will hurt. Your settlement itself is never wiped out, so you can always rebuild.

Are these pictures from the game?

Not yet. They are concept art that shows the world and the mood we are building. The finished game may look different.
