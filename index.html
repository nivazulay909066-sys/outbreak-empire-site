The old world is gone.
Build the next one.

The outbreak took the cities, the governments and everything that held them together. What remains is scattered across the ruins: survivors looking for somewhere safe, rivals looking for something to take, and the dead.

You lead a small band of survivors with a chance to build something that lasts. A refuge, a trading power, or an empire.

What it becomes is up to you.
