Our promise

Not pay to win. Play to win. Pay to win Play to win

Outbreak Empire is made for players who are done with strategy games where you have to pay hundreds of dollars a month just to keep up. The game has no in‑app purchases. What you achieve depends only on your own skill.

  • No in-app purchases

    Nothing inside the game is for sale. No packs, no gems, no VIP levels, no shortcuts.

  • Your moves decide

    Every building, every army and every win comes from how you plan, fight and lead.

  • Same rules for all

    No one can buy their way to the top of the map. The strongest player is the best player, not the one who spent the most.

The world

The city fell. The dead took it all. Now lead. Build a new order.

No one knows how it started. One thing is certain: the world will never be what it was. The government and the army were the first to fall, and the old order fell with them.

What is left is a world of groups and settlements fighting to survive, gangs and militias that thrive on the chaos, and hordes of the dead that turn toward every sound.

You lead a small settlement facing countless dangers and chances. Everything that happens from now on is on you.

A refuge. A fortress. An empire.

FAQ

Questions

What kind of game is Outbreak Empire?

A post-apocalyptic zombie strategy game for Android and iOS, built on pure play to win. You build and grow your base, a settlement in a ruined city, take in survivors and put them to work, hold your walls against the hordes, and defend against or attack other players who want what is yours. It is a multiplayer strategy game: join a coalition and plan your wars together.

When does Outbreak Empire come out?

There is no launch date yet. A closed test comes first. Join the waitlist and we will email you as soon as there is news.

Which devices will it run on?

Outbreak Empire is a post-apocalyptic mobile game, being made for Android and iOS.

Will it be free?

Yes. Outbreak Empire will be free, with no in-app purchases at all.

Is Outbreak Empire pay to win?

No. It is play to win. Nothing in the game is for sale, so no one can buy power, speed or resources. The top of the map belongs to the players who play best.

What is the closed test?

An early version of the game, played by a limited group before launch so we can find problems and tune the balance. We will invite players from the waitlist. Joining the waitlist does not guarantee a place.

Do I play alone?

No. Every settlement shares the world with real players. Defend against them, attack them, or join a coalition and take on what no single settlement could.

Are these pictures from the game?

Not yet. They are concept art that shows the world and the mood we are building. The finished game will look different.
